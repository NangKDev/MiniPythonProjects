# Included projects

1. PigGame.py (Pig Dice Game)

    A multiplayer, turn-based console dice game built in Python.
    
    * Players: 2 or more players take turns rolling a single 6-sided die.
    * Turn Rules:
      * Each roll adds the die value to your current turn's score.
      * Roll a 1: You lose all points accumulated in that round, and your turn ends immediately.
      * Hold / End Turn: You can choose to stop rolling at any point to lock in and bank your round score to your total.
    * Winning Condition:
      * The first player to reach or exceed the maximum target score wins.
      * Round Completion: The game ensures round fairness by allowing all players (up to the final player) to complete their turn before the final winner is declared.
